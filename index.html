Meet the magician

Your delivery guy has a few tricks up his sleeve

Nathaniel Segal is a seasoned professional magician and consultant who spends as much time behind the scenes as he does in front of audiences. Nathaniel has been featured on Penn & Teller: Fool Us, Masters of Illusion, and has headlined at the Magic Castle.

Nathaniel has also served as a magic consultant for David Blaine’s Las Vegas show, America’s Got Talent semifinals, and the Impossible Science program. He currently has a monthly immersive magic show at Illusion Magic Lounge in Santa Monica.
